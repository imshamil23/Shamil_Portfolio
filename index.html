01 — About

Spreadsheets are my raw material.

I'm a data analyst based in Kerala, currently working as an Automation Analyst at AM Motors, a 25+ branch Maruti Suzuki dealership network. I got promoted out of my original Data Analyst role within nine months — not because I chased the title, but because I kept finding manual reporting work that shouldn't have been manual, and fixing it.

My comfort zone is Excel, VBA and Power Query — the unglamorous layer that takes messy, branch-level exports and turns them into something a Power BI dashboard can actually trust. I'm equally at home writing a macro that saves someone an hour a day, or explaining to a Sales Head why a number moved.

Right now I'm deliberately building out my SQL and Python skills so I can push further upstream — closer to the database, not just the spreadsheet that was pulled from it.
